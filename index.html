Know the Path

What Each Path Generally Focuses On

A quick introduction before you explore specific courses.

STEM

Focuses on science, mathematics, technology, engineering, health, architecture, and related areas.

HUMSS

Focuses on people, society, communication, language, culture, politics, education, and public service.

ABM

Focuses on accounting, business, finance, entrepreneurship, marketing, economics, and management.

GAS

Provides a broad academic direction for students who are still considering different college possibilities.

TVL

Focuses on practical, technical, vocational, livelihood, and hands-on skills.

Arts & Design

Focuses on creative expression, visual arts, media, writing, communication, and design.

Sports

Focuses on physical activity, fitness, sports, wellness, coaching, and teaching.

TechPro

Emphasizes technical and practical preparation, skills development, employment, certification, and entrepreneurship pathways.
