Why course mismatch happens

A better decision starts with awareness.

Course mismatch can happen for different reasons. PATHFINDER encourages open discussion, self-understanding, and better information.

01

Parental pressure

A parent may prefer a particular course without fully knowing the student's own interests. Comparing perspectives can open a respectful conversation.

02

Lack of information

A student may feel lost because they do not yet know what courses involve or what careers they can lead to. Course and career guides help fill that gap.

03

Peer pressure

A student may lean toward a course because friends are choosing it. A personal assessment helps them pause and consider what fits their own interests.

Important: An assessment result is a starting point for reflection, not a final decision or a psychological diagnosis. The student's voice should remain central.
