UNDERSTAND BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Two Perspectives. One Conversation.

The parent and student answer different questions. Their answers use the same interest categories so the results can be compared fairly.

👨‍👩‍👧
FOR PARENTS OR GUARDIANS

How Well Do You Know Your Child?

Answer based on what you have actually observed about your child. Do not answer only according to the course you want them to take.

  • Observe your child's interests.
  • Identify activities they enjoy.
  • Recognize strengths you have noticed.
  • Choose "Haven't observed" when unsure.
🧭
FOR STUDENTS

Discover Your Own Interests

Answer honestly based on what you personally enjoy, want to learn, and would choose even without pressure from other people.

  • Reflect on your personal interests.
  • Identify activities you enjoy.
  • Discover your possible strengths.
  • Reflect on influences affecting your decisions.
Important: These assessments are educational guides, not validated psychological tests. Different answers do not mean that a parent or student is wrong. They are an opportunity for an honest conversation.

UNDERSTANDING COURSE MISMATCH

Why Do Students Choose Courses That May Not Fit Them?

PATHFINDER encourages students to understand their interests and make informed decisions.

01

Parental Pressure

Students may feel pushed toward a course chosen by family expectations instead of their own interests.

02

Lack of Information

Students may not fully understand what a course involves or what career opportunities it can lead to.

03

Peer Pressure

Students may choose a course because their friends are choosing it, even if another field interests them more.
