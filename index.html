Know Before You Choose

What Each Path Generally Focuses On

A simple starting point before exploring individual courses.

STEM

Science, mathematics, technology, engineering, health, architecture, and related fields.

ABM

Business, accounting, finance, entrepreneurship, marketing, economics, and management.

HUMSS

People, society, communication, language, culture, education, politics, and public service.

GAS

A broad academic direction for students exploring different college possibilities.

TVL

Hands-on, technical, vocational, practical, livelihood, and career-oriented skills.

Arts & Design

Visual arts, communication, creativity, design, media, and artistic expression.

Sports

Physical activity, sports, wellness, fitness, health, and teaching.

TechPro

Practical and technical preparation connected to employment, skills, certification, and entrepreneurship.
