Traditional Strand Overview

What Each Strand Focuses On

A simple overview to help students understand the general direction of each traditional strand.

STEM

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics

For students interested in science, mathematics, technology, engineering, health, architecture, and related fields.

HUMSS

Humanities and Social Sciences

For students interested in people, society, communication, language, culture, politics, public service, and education.

ABM

Accountancy, Business, and Management

For students interested in business, accounting, finance, entrepreneurship, marketing, economics, and management.

GAS

General Academic Strand

A broad strand for students who are still exploring different college directions and want flexibility in choosing future programs.

TVL

Technical-Vocational-Livelihood

Focuses on practical, technical, vocational, and livelihood-related skills. It includes areas such as ICT, Home Economics, Industrial Arts, and Agri-Fishery Arts.

ARTS

Arts and Design

Covers creative fields such as theater, music, dance, creative writing, visual arts, media arts, communication, and design.

SPORTS

Sports Track

Focuses on physical education, sports, fitness, wellness, sports science, coaching, and related areas.

TECHPRO

Technical Professional

A current strengthened-SHS pathway emphasizing practical, technical, and career-oriented learning, including skills, certifications, employment, and entrepreneurship pathways.

STEM

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics
